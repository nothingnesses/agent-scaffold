### `workflow-audit-followups`: the 2026-08-13 audit's remaining recommendations, held as one deferred pointer rather than a backlog

Deferred, deliberately, and deliberately EMPTY of PLAN content. It holds a pointer and, since 2026-08-14, a record of measurements. It holds no work items.

The 2026-08-13 audit made ten ordered recommendations. Recommendation 1 is the release, which is `ship-v0-0-2`. Recommendations 2 to 10 stay where they were written: `docs/plans/workflow-calibration.explorations/2026-08-13-audit-when-the-loop-turned.md`, under `## Recommendations`, with the measurements that justify each of them in the sections above it. They are not copied here, not split into steps, and not summarised.

WHY THIS STEP IS A POINTER AND NOT A PLAN. The same audit measured that the share of this project's steps whose provenance is its own review process rose from 8.3% to 54.2%, monotonically, and that 74.3% of 1,022 commits touched neither product code nor tests. Turning ten recommendations into ten steps would be that mechanism running once more, at the moment the evidence for it arrived. One deferred pointer keeps them findable without creating work that then generates its own review rounds.

WHEN TO OPEN IT. After `v0.0.2` is published and `rename-to-agent-flow` has run, and then only against evidence from use rather than against the recommendations' own ordering. Read the audit record first, in full, and pick from it; do not treat this step as a queue to drain (Principle 2, Minimal by default; Principle 6, Ground decisions in evidence).

ONE THING TO CARRY FORWARD when it is opened, because it is the audit's own assessment of leverage and it is cheap to lose: recommendation 3, constraining what a fix pass may do, is the single highest-leverage variable the audit found. Deletion-only and number-edit-only fix passes re-seed findings at close to zero; fix passes that author prose re-seed at close to 100%. The project had measured that five separate times without acting on it. THAT SECOND SENTENCE IS THE AUDIT'S FRAMING AND IT IS CORRECTED TWICE BELOW, so do not stop at the first correction: mechanism 3 measured the same partition one level down and found it falls elsewhere, and mechanism 4 then measured a pass that authored no content word at all and still took a finding at each site it edited, which retires the authoring variable itself. Mechanism 4 is the one to apply.

MECHANISM 1, DOMAIN WIDENING WITHOUT A CONSUMER RE-CHECK (round 2, `d7c9fb0`). The round 1 fix pass made `ReadError::Escapes` producible for ANY string, where before it was producible only for an absolute or `..`-bearing one. Two callers that discarded the error, `src/main.rs:229-233` (`Err(_) => Ok(Vec::new())`) and `src/main.rs:259` (`.unwrap_or_default()`), were CORRECT BEFORE THE WIDENING AND WRONG AFTER IT: a containment refusal became indistinguishable from "the pack ships no such file", so a run wrote a materially wrong `AGENTS.md` at exit 0 with empty stderr, byte-identical to a legitimate one. Two comments elsewhere, `src/manifest.rs:429-430` and `:485-487`, became false without being edited. THE FIX PASS'S OWN NEW TEXT WAS TRUE: the claims reviewer checked its four rewritten claim sites and found all four true, the triager re-ran three of them, and exactly one new false statement was authored by the pass, a `low`, false only on a platform this environment cannot run. So the triage's own conclusion is that a claims lens over a fix pass's new text could not have found the round's `high`, and what finds it is enumerating the CONSUMERS of any value whose domain the change widens, then running the pre-change and post-change binaries against shapes outside the existing test set. The same triage named a twin form, INPUT-SET WIDENING WITHOUT RE-ENUMERATING: the refusal's input set grew from strings a pack author writes deliberately to any file a deployment tool happens to link, and the pass disclosed the two shapes it tested and enumerated none it did not. THE DELTA AGAINST THE AUDIT: the triage states in terms that this is not the mechanism the audit named. Its own attribution shows why a constraint on what a fix pass may AUTHOR does not reach this shape. The round's `high` is a BEHAVIOUR the pass introduced, plus two pre-existing comments it invalidated without touching them, and one of the round's `low`s (`claims 2`) is that same invalidation shape on five more sites.

MECHANISM 2, THE UNMEASURED COMPARATIVE CLAIM (round 3, `306d13a`). Six of round 3's eight reviewer findings (nine valid in total, counting `Z1`, which the triager raised itself) were new prose of one grammatical shape: "X now, where before or elsewhere it was Y", with the Y half never run. The triage tabulated each with the single command that falsifies it: run the previous release's binary; run the pre-change suite under the over-tightening; run a pack with both literals linked; run `validate --workflow` with an absolute and a `..`-bearing `--metrics`; grep for a join in `plan::source`; write a swallowing caller. Not one was run. Its summary is that the pass verified what it BUILT and asserted what it REPLACED. THE SECOND-ORDER DRIVER, which that triage calls the most useful thing in its document: the false sentences trace back to the PREVIOUS ROUND'S TRIAGE remedy text, carried into the tree. `X3` is a near-verbatim carry of `v002-r2-triage.md:363`, "nothing currently pins it at any level", an assertion that triage made without running the pre-change suite. `X5` and `X6` are the two artefacts round 2's `B2` remedy asked for by name, and that remedy's pointer at `README.md:242` is what produced the false "the same rule". `X1` is the residue of round 2's `claims 3` remedy, which scoped the fix to a parenthetical and did not check the clause the parenthetical sat inside. A remedy stated as an assertion becomes a claim in the tree, and the next round falsifies the claim, so the review loop is a source of its own next round's findings. One caution for a reader of the source: that section counts these as "THREE of the six" while its own list names four sites (`X1`, `X3`, `X5`, `X6`). The four sites are the measured part.

MECHANISM 3, THE DELETION-VERSUS-AUTHORING PARTITION (round 4, `01333f3`). THIS IS THE ONE THAT CORRECTS THE AUDIT. Round 3's fix pass (`fde1d60`) was a deletion pass by instruction. The triager partitioned its edit sites by what the pass DID at each one, attributed every finding of the round to a site with `git log -S`, and confirmed each against three separately built release binaries (HEAD, the pre-fix tree, and `main` at 0.0.1), verified distinct by `md5sum`. NINE SITES:

- FOUR PURE-DELETION SITES: two `CHANGELOG.md` bullets removed with one sentence moved verbatim, `README.md:325`, `src/safe_path.rs:1-7`, and `tests/...:383-385`. ZERO findings between them.
- FIVE SITES WHERE THE PASS AUTHORED SOMETHING: `src/manifest.rs:1319-1320` (delete, then write a shorter replacement) 0 findings; `tests/...:445-469` (write a new test and its comment) 0; `src/manifest.rs:524-528` (delete, then write a replacement clause) 1; `CHANGELOG.md:37` (delete, then write a replacement bullet) 1; `src/main.rs:2110-2124` (write new code, a comment and a message) 2. FOUR findings in total.

SO EVERY FINDING OF THE ROUND SITS AT A SITE WHERE THE PASS AUTHORED SOMETHING, AND NO PURE-DELETION SITE PRODUCED ONE. The implication runs one way only: two of the five authored sites produced nothing, and one produced two, so an authored site is not a finding each. One arithmetic caution for a reader of the source: its prose says "seven edit sites" while its own table lists nine, and nine is what the table's own counts add to (4 plus 5).

THE DELETIONS THEMSELVES WORKED, measured in the same round. Every string round 3 falsified is gone, checked per string at HEAD and at `HEAD~1`, and no replacement inherited the falsity, checked replacement by replacement. Four of the five NEW comparative claims the pass authored hold, each run by the triager, so the falsification rate on newly authored comparative claims fell from 6 of 6 to 1 of 5.

THE CORRECTION TO THE AUDIT'S RECOMMENDATION 3, STATED EXPLICITLY. The audit (`docs/plans/workflow-calibration.explorations/2026-08-13-audit-when-the-loop-turned.md`, recommendation 3 and Condition 2) says to constrain what a fix pass may do, on the measurement that deletion-only and number-edit-only fixes re-seed at close to zero while fix passes that author prose re-seed at close to 100%. That framing puts the variable on the PASS. Round 4 measured it on the SITE, and the partition does not fall where the audit puts it: `fde1d60` was a deletion pass and it still produced four findings at a re-seeding rate of 80%, and TWO OF THE FOUR ARE AT SITES WHERE THE PASS DELETED AND THEN WROTE A REPLACEMENT, which the audit's framing would score on the deletion side. The operative constraint is therefore not "delete rather than rewrite". It is whether the pass AUTHORS A REPLACEMENT SENTENCE AT ALL, at any site. Both records are cited so a reader can check the correction rather than take it: the audit at the path above, and `v002-r4-triage.md` at `01333f3`, under "The correction, and what it implies for a fifth round".

WHAT THE ROUND 4 TRIAGER DREW FROM THAT MEASUREMENT, recorded as its inference and not as a measurement: every remaining edit must be a deletion, and where a claim cannot be deleted outright, its replacement must be copied verbatim from a measurement already in a findings file. Its stated reason a deletion-only rule cannot stand alone is that a pass cannot delete its way to a new error message or a new `CHANGELOG.md` bullet.

MECHANISM 4, THE SURVIVING TEXT RE-SCOPED BY WHAT WAS DELETED (round 6, `7ed86f8`). THIS IS THE FOURTH MEASUREMENT OF THIS MECHANISM AND ITS TRIAGER SAYS SO IN ITS OWN TEXT. Read this record first, ahead of the three above it, for the reason the supersession paragraph below gives. Its subject is the deletion cut the human authorised at the cap escalation. The triager extracted the 0.0.2 release section pre-cut and post-cut and tested every surviving bullet for byte-identity against its pre-cut form: 21 bullets before and 15 after, made of 7 deleted outright, 12 untouched survivors, 2 rewritten in place and 1 authored new. FINDINGS BY CLASS: the 7 whole-bullet deletions produced ZERO between them; the 2 partial-sentence edits raised 3 and produced 2 valid; the 1 authored bullet produced 1; and the 1 finding at an untouched survivor sits on a bullet byte-identical pre-cut and post-cut, so it predates the cut rather than being caused by it.

WHAT IT FALSIFIES, MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED. The triager ran a per-bullet word diff on the two rewritten bullets. One deleted 66 words and added two, the other deleted 55 and added three, and ALL FIVE ADDITIONS ARE PUNCTUATION OR CAPITALISATION REPAIRS AT A CUT SEAM: a mid-sentence clause became a sentence start and took a capital, a semicolon-joined half was removed so a semicolon became a full stop, and a list lost its third item so the conjunction before the second became a comma. NOT ONE NEW CONTENT WORD WAS AUTHORED INTO EITHER BULLET, AND BOTH STILL PRODUCED FINDINGS. So the findings did not come from authored text.

THE ACTUAL MECHANISM. A partial cut silently RE-SCOPES the text that survives, because quantifiers, anaphors, definite articles and contrast structures in the surviving text were calibrated against the text that was removed. The round's own three instances: a definite plural plus a two-item apposition that still read as a complete enumeration after the count word and one list item went; an anaphor ("neither") whose antecedent pair lost a member; and an outcome description that was disambiguated by a following sentence which the cut deleted. Deleting a WHOLE unit cannot do this, because nothing inside it survives to be re-scoped.

THE CHECK THAT FOLLOWS, and it caught a live instance in the very pass that adopted it: after a partial cut, re-read the whole surviving unit and resolve every "neither", "both", "each", "the two", "these" and "it" in it against what is actually still there. A whole-unit deletion carries a different obligation, the inbound-reference sweep below.

HOW MECHANISM 4 SUPERSEDES THE THREE ABOVE, STATED EXPLICITLY BECAUSE A READER WHO APPLIES ALL FOUR GETS A CONSTRAINT THAT HAS SINCE BEEN MEASURED WRONG. The audit's recommendation 3 puts the variable on what a fix pass AUTHORS. Mechanism 3 sharpened that onto the SITE and concluded that the operative constraint is whether the pass authors a replacement sentence at all. Round 6 measured a pass that authored no content word at either rewritten site and still took a finding at each, so THE AUTHORING VARIABLE IS NOT WHAT SEPARATES THE SAFE CLASS FROM THE UNSAFE ONE. What survives is WHOLE-UNIT DELETION VERSUS ANY EDIT INSIDE A UNIT, whether that edit authors anything or not. Two things are NOT overturned and must not be read as retired: mechanism 3's site-level partition, whose measurement stands and only whose stated variable is replaced; and mechanisms 1 and 2, each of which names a different shape rather than a version of the authoring model. Apply mechanism 4. Read the other three as the history of how it was reached.

THE INBOUND-REFERENCE SWEEP, PROVED BY THE TARGETED VERIFICATION THAT FOLLOWED ROUND 6 (record 343, `e82e303`). It is recorded here rather than as a fifth mechanism because a different pass proved it and because it is the obligation a WHOLE-unit deletion carries, where mechanism 4's check is the obligation a partial cut carries.

WHAT HAPPENED. The round 6 fix deleted, as a whole sentence, the claim its triage had ruled the release section's only false statement of fact. THE SAME SENTENCE SURVIVED VERBATIM IN `README.md`, which `Cargo.toml`'s `readme` field ships inside the published crate and which crates.io renders as the crate's front page. Six rounds missed it, across 6,430 lines in the eighteen reviewer and triage files those rounds produced, because every round read the `CHANGELOG` and none searched the repository for the deleted sentence's own phrases. The verifier found it with one `grep -nE` for six distinctive phrases over three files, then confirmed it reaches users by grepping the packaged tarball. The same sweep also found the claim in the `audit` subcommand's clap doc comment in `src/main.rs`, WHICH SHIPS COMPILED INTO THE BINARY.

THE GAP WAS COVERAGE AND NOT RIGOUR, which is why further rounds of the same shape would not have closed it. THE DURABLE RULE: after deleting a sentence, grep two or three of its distinctive phrases across the whole repository rather than only the file you edited, and include whatever a package manifest ships. It is one command and it is not a reviewer's judgement call.

THE PER-ROUND RE-SEEDING RATES. Three triagers measured this, each on its own attribution, and THE DENOMINATORS DIFFER, so each is given with its own.

- ROUND 2 (`d7c9fb0`): 3 of 6 VALID FINDINGS strict, 50%, where strict means text or behaviour the fix pass INTRODUCED. 4 of 6 broad, 67%, where broad adds findings whose subject the pass did not write but did make false. Weighted by severity, the pass owned the round's only `high` and only `medium`.
- ROUND 3 (`306d13a`): 7 of 8 REVIEWER FINDINGS strict, 88%, and 7 of 8 broad. A third figure the triage added because round 2's two definitions do not cover the shape it found, "text this pass wrote OR rewrote": 8 of 8. The denominator excludes the ninth valid finding of the round, which the triager raised itself and attributed to the round 1 pass. Weighted by severity the picture inverts: seven `low`s, and no defect of any severity in the code the pass changed.
- ROUND 4 (`01333f3`): 4 of 5 strict, 80%, counting defect SITES rather than findings so the numbers compare, because one finding was merged from two reviewers and carries two halves.
- The audit's project-wide baseline, for comparison: 49% strict and 61% broad, across 189 of 211 round-2-onward findings.

Round 4 stated the series and what it shows. 50%, then 88%, then 80%: THE RATE IS FLAT WITHIN NOISE AND THE ABSOLUTE COUNT FELL, from 8 findings with 7 re-seeded to 5 findings with 4 re-seeded.

ROUND 6 IS ABSENT FROM THAT SERIES DELIBERATELY, AND ITS ABSENCE IS NOT A GAP TO FILL. Its triage partitioned the cut by SITE CLASS and did not compute a strict or broad rate, so putting it on this axis would mean deriving a figure its own measurement does not support. Its partition is under mechanism 4 above, and mechanism 4 is why the axis this series measures is the wrong one anyway.

THE CAUSATION QUESTION IS OPEN AND IS DELIBERATELY NOT ANSWERED HERE (`Q-76`, human, 2026-08-14). ## The orchestrator-defect pattern, and the countermeasure that worked

CAPTURED 2026-08-14, AS ONE PATTERN AND NOT AS SEVEN ITEMS, because the pattern is the finding and the individual defects are its evidence. Like the mechanisms above, this is measurement. It adds no work item to this step.

THE PATTERN: THE ORCHESTRATOR PRODUCES CONFIDENT SUMMARIES FASTER THAN IT VERIFIES THEM, AND NOTHING IN THIS WORKFLOW CHECKS A SUMMARY BEFORE IT BECOMES A BRIEF OR A DECISION. Principle 6 (Ground decisions in evidence) is the principle it breaks, and it breaks it at the handover rather than at the gathering: a summary is what a human decides on and what a writer builds from, and this workflow runs a review loop over the artifact while running nothing at all over the account of it. The 2026-08-13 audit named this in the orchestrator's own conduct before any of the defects below happened.

THE EVIDENCE IS IN THE LEDGER, BY DEFECT NUMBER, AND IS NOT COPIED HERE. Orchestrator defects (26) to (31) are in the paragraph beginning "ORCHESTRATOR DEFECTS (26) TO (31), RECORDED TOGETHER BECAUSE THEY ARE ONE PATTERN", and defect (32) is in the paragraph beginning "ORCHESTRATOR DEFECT (32), AND IT WAS OVERTURNED BY THE WRITER I BRIEFED WITH IT". Find them by that text rather than by position. Their shapes run from citing reproductions that existed only in an agent's returned report, through briefing a fix pass from a paraphrase naming one call site where the class had two, to five relayed figures that downstream agents falsified. One of them chained a harvest copy and a `git worktree remove --force` with `;`, and when the copy failed a precondition the removal destroyed both reviewers' findings files, which is the same class as the scratch-directory rule the orchestrator hands to every agent.

DEFECT (32) IS THE ONE TO ACT ON, BECAUSE IT ARRIVED WITH A COUNTERMEASURE THAT WORKED AND THAT COSTS ONE SENTENCE. The orchestrator measured a claim about the published 0.0.1 six times, on six fixtures that all ran the same masked path, and took the wrong conclusion to the human, who decided on it. Every repetition raised its confidence and not one of them changed the masking. THE WRITER BRIEFED WITH THAT CONCLUSION OVERTURNED IT, and the remedy shrank from deleting a sentence to narrowing three words inside it. The only reason it was caught is one sentence in the brief licensing the writer to contradict the orchestrator FIRST AND PROMINENTLY, given alongside the statement that the human had already decided the other way.

THE COUNTERMEASURE, STATED AS THE THING TO ADOPT: a writer handed a conclusion must be told that the conclusion may be wrong and that a correction outranks a clean report. It is one sentence in a brief and it needs no tooling. It does not replace the roles that already catch this class from outside, since defects (24) and (26) were both caught by a planner reading the orchestrator's own reasoning. What it adds is that the agent ALREADY HOLDING THE EVIDENCE is told it may contradict the brief, instead of reading the brief as the specification to satisfy.
